A guide to the evidence

Four lenses.
Different measures.

The same person can watch both AFL and NRL, use several video platforms and attend multiple games. These are different units: audience reach, weekly platform use, exclusive-live broadcast windows and match entries. Each section asks a distinct question, without mixing the totals.

03 / MEDIA HABITS

How are viewing habits changing?

All-video market context

Video viewing has fragmented across paid streaming, free-to-air and catch-up. These ACMA results cover all video, not just sport.

Important distinction: The ACMA charts below describe all video viewing by Australian adults, not sport-specific television audiences. Different media categories can overlap: one person can use several services.

Figure 6 / Streaming, broadcast and short-form video · Advanced slope chart

Broadcast TV recovered in 2025; paid streaming held steady

Compare all four media types in the ACMA 2024 and 2025 adult surveys. The y-axis shows the share of Australians who used each type of video service during the previous seven days, not time spent watching.

Key finding: Traditional free-to-air television rose from 46% to 52% (+6 percentage points), while paid streaming remained at 68% and short-form video fell from 59% to 52% (−7 points).

Source: ACMA, How we watch and listen to content (March 2026), executive summary. Base: Australian adults, past seven days. Media types overlap; percentage-point changes are not audience counts.

Figure 14 / Crowd inequality · Advanced Lorenz concentration curve

How unequal are AFL stadium crowds, really?

The teal curve accumulates stadium crowds from the smallest to the largest of 18 venues. The dashed diagonal is the equal-distribution reference. The farther the curve bends below it, the more crowded the leading venues become.

Key finding: The three busiest grounds alone captured 68.8% of recorded 2025 crowd entries. The venue-concentration Gini coefficient is 0.72; the zero-to-one measure is descriptive, not a ranking of stadium quality.

Source: AFL Tables, 2025 attendance by venue, all 18 venues included. Each venue appears once. Curve and Gini calculated from exact season totals, including finals.

The final whistle / What the evidence says

Viewing, access and attendance aren't the same contest.

National TV reach, general video-platform use and stadium crowd entries measure different behaviours. Rights vary by postcode, fixtures influence the biggest annual crowds, and the per-game leaderboard looks different from the total-attendance leaderboard.
